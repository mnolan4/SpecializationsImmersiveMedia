Faculty working draft · September 2026

Depth, without closing the experiment.

The Specializations Committee is determining how a student who finds an area of immersive-media practice can go further in it, while the program remains a place to wander, combine practices, and change direction.

These pages summarize the published curriculum and the committee’s current-state reading. Faculty validation sheets and the student experience worksheet have not yet been returned. Nothing here adopts a new course, a prerequisite change, or a transcript label.

How can students turn a collection of courses into a coherent creative, technical, and professional trajectory?

That question holds the committee’s charge together: a definition of specialization, pilot readings of game design and artificial intelligence, and the continuity of work from Collaborative Studio I through Collaborative Studio II and Senior Capstone.

Computing and software

Established depth on the Computing B.S., through the computer-science core, upper-level electives, and IMDM327.

Studio and digital practice

Established depth on the Emerging Creatives B.A., through studio foundations, digital media, and IMDM350.

Collaborative production

The shared spine. IMDM101, IMDM227, IMDM290, IMDM390, and Senior Capstone. This is the ground of a specialization, not a specialization of its own.

Where the sequence divides

Both degree tracks require IMDM290, IMDM390, IMDM490, and IMDM491. The four-year plans place them in that order. Registration does not. IMDM490 requires IMDM290, major standing, and 86 credits. It does not require IMDM390. IMDM390 is spring-only and IMDM490 is fall-only, so a student who begins Capstone first often takes Collaborative Studio II in the last semester, concurrent with IMDM491 and the public showcase. Skills learned in that studio then have no later course in which to enter the senior project, or to be shown as advanced understanding.

The diagram distinguishes the advised studio sequence from the shorter path the registration rules allow. On that shorter path, IMDM390 often shares the last semester with Capstone II and the showcase. The full account is on the continuity page.

Areas present as pieces

Game design, extended reality, artificial intelligence, interaction, physical computing, three-dimensional and technical art, sound, and creative research are already in the curriculum as courses, facilities, and faculty expertise. A student who wants to go further still has to assemble the route. Under the committee’s definition, that is a cluster until the path itself is findable, developmental, open, continuous, shown in a body of work, and taught on purpose inside IMD.

Definition

Six conditions, all required. The shared spine, the two degree tracks, and program-wide competencies such as collaboration stay outside the definition.

Known gaps

Breaks on an ordinary path through the published major, and breaks the rules still allow when a student’s route is less common.

Working papers

Game design

A pilot case. The courses, facilities, and expertise are present. The path a student can follow is not yet supplied by the curriculum.

Artificial intelligence

A second pilot, read across both degree tracks, for students who want to build, critique, and shape intelligent media.
