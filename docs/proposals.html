What is emerging · September 2026

Proposals, and what is not yet one.

One scheduling proposal is on the table. The definition and the studio-sequence analysis are the committee’s working instruments. They are not adopted curriculum.

Draft proposal

Offer IMDM227 in fall and spring

Treat IMDM227 as the first gateway to expand. Consider IMDM290 only if, after that change, it is the next bottleneck. Do not assume both courses must run every semester at once. Staffing, enrollment, facilities, and the program’s ability to keep upper-level electives are part of the proposal.

Why this matters for students. Transfer students, students changing direction, and students who miss a spring currently wait out a year at the front of the sequence. The wait is not confined to one course.

Under discussion

Studio II and Capstone

The published structure is clear: IMDM490 does not require IMDM390, and the calendar often places 390 beside the showcase. The committee has not recommended that 390 become a prerequisite, or what an individual student should carry from one studio to the next.

Why this matters for students. A repair to registration order and a repair to handoff do different work. Students need both the experiential studio before the project is defined, and a way for earlier work to remain usable.
