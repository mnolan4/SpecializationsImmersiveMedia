Curriculum and pathways

Paths a student can follow.

A pathway is a visible route from an introduction, through repeated practice, to advanced work and a body of work. The two degree tracks are how students enter IMD. They are not specializations inside it.

Game design

Mechanics, prototyping, and playtesting do not yet have a shared introduction both tracks can enter. CMSC425 is the clearest advanced course, and it is most accessible after the upper-level Computing sequence.

Why this matters for students. A student on the Emerging Creatives track can meet games in studios and in IMDM350, and still lack a stated route into the advanced course.

Artificial intelligence

There is no shared AI foundation designed for both tracks. The clearest technical sequences require substantial computing prerequisites. Creative and critical work is distributed across courses. It is not yet a progression.

Why this matters for students. Access to relevant CMSC coursework, and what a student can do without that coursework, has to be part of the pathway rather than an unspoken extra.

What students meet before they choose

IMDM101 is where students first encounter the field.
