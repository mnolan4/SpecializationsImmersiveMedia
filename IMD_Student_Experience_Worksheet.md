# IMD Student Experience Worksheet

**Specializations Committee**  
**September 2026**

The fillable form is `IMD_Student_Experience_Worksheet.pdf`. Students should be able to finish it in one sitting.

The committee reads the sheets for patterns, beside the faculty validation sheets. Interest shows demand. It does not by itself make an area a specialization. A gap or a break is compared with what instructors report. This worksheet does not set requirements, transcript labels, or course redesigns.

Individual sheets stay with the committee. Reports to faculty share patterns and omit student names. The purpose, use, and privacy language below is what students see on the form.

**None**, **Still looking**, and **Not yet** are finished answers.

---

# For students

> **Purpose.** This survey asks how you experience the IMD major: where you want to go further, where a course needed something you did not yet have, how the curriculum feels, and whether later courses use earlier work. Exploring, and caring about more than one area, are useful answers.
>
> **Use.** The IMD Specializations Committee will read the responses together and look for patterns. It will use those patterns, along with reports from faculty about the courses they teach, in its discussion of specializations and of how courses connect. Your answers are not a grade. They do not change your schedule or your degree requirements.
>
> **Privacy.** Your name is optional. If you leave it blank, the committee will not try to identify you. If you write your name, the committee can connect this sheet to you. Only committee members will read individual sheets. In any report to faculty, the committee will share patterns and will not include your name. A project or other detail you choose to describe can still make a response recognizable. Do not name other students.

Where a question says check all, mark every box that fits. Where it says choose one, mark a single answer.
